# Ranking for “dota hacks”, “dota 2 hacks”, cheats & aimbot

Technical SEO in this repo (meta, schema, sitemap, internal links, FAQ) is necessary but **not sufficient** for #1 worldwide. Google ranks pages that win on **relevance, authority, and user signals**—not keyword count alone.

## On-site (already in the template)

- Edit **`src/config/pageSeo.ts`** and **`src/config/blogSeo.ts`** when Search Console shows new queries.
- Keep **`SEO_YEAR`** in **`src/config/site.ts`** current.
- One canonical domain in **`SITE_DOMAIN`**; verify in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Submit **`/sitemap.xml`** after every deploy; set **`INDEXNOW_KEY`** for faster Bing/Yandex discovery.
- Use **`docs/search-console.md`** monthly to tune titles from impression data.

## Off-site (you must do this for top rankings)

1. **Backlinks** — editorial links from gaming/tech sites, YouTube descriptions, and communities (quality over quantity).
2. **Brand searches** — people searching your brand + “dota 2 hacks” signals trust; Discord, support, and consistent naming help.
3. **Content velocity** — new patch-day blog posts beat stale competitors; link them from the homepage topic hub.
4. **Avoid duplicate sites** — one live domain; no copied content on multiple URLs.
5. **Core Web Vitals** — keep Lighthouse performance strong (images are WebP, fonts self-hosted).

## Realistic expectations

Head terms like “dota 2 hacks” and “dota cheats” are highly competitive. Plan on **months** of content + links, then refine titles/descriptions from **Performance → Queries** in Search Console—not guesswork.
